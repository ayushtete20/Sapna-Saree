Our Story

Rooted in Heritage,
Crafted with Purpose

Sapna Sarees by Lavichitra was born from a dream — to bring the finest handwoven sarees from India's legendary weaving centres into the hands of women who appreciate true craftsmanship.

Founded in 2010, we work directly with master weavers in Varanasi, Kanchipuram, and Chanderi — ensuring fair wages, preserving ancient techniques, and bringing you pieces that carry the soul of centuries of Indian textile artistry.
